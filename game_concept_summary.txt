Beyond All Reason (BAR) is a free, open-source real-time strategy (RTS) game built in the spirit of classic large-scale RTS titles like Total Annihilation and Supreme Commander. It pits massive armies against each other in fast-paced, high-apriori warfare featuring thousands of physical, simulated units on the battlefield at once.

---

### Core Gameplay Dynamics & Economy
* Streamlined Streaming Economy: BAR uses a dual-resource system—Metal (mined from specific spots or reclaimed from destroyed wreckages) and Energy (generated via wind turbines, solar panels, or reactors). Resources stream in real-time rather than being banked outright.
* Scavenging & Reclaiming: Wrecked vehicles and destroyed bases leave metal debris on the field. Reclaiming front-line battlefields with construction units often shifts the economic tides faster than mining fresh nodes.
* Tech Scaling: Battles start with nimble Tier 1 (T1) raiding forces and commanders, scaling into heavy Tier 2 (T2) armored pushes, and culminating in Tier 3 (T3) experimental juggernauts, nuclear silos, and long-range orbital weaponry.

---

### Deep Strategy in High-Level Competitive Play
In high-level team matches (such as standard 8v8 lobbies) and competitive 1v1 play, strategy moves beyond basic base-building into multi-layered coordination:

1. Role Specialization in 8v8:
   * Frontline/Lanes: Players manage immediate lane pressure, trading metal efficiently and holding choke points.
   * Eco/Tech Rushing: Teammates in the backline sacrifice early combat power to rush high-tier energy plants (e.g., Fusion) to feed metal convertors, enabling rapid T2/T3 unit spam for the team.
   * Air & Naval Support: Specialized air commanders build fighter screens and bomber wings, while naval commanders control water lanes to dominate coastal bombardments.

2. Advanced Physics & Simulation Mechanics:
   * Line of Sight & Radar Jammers: Radar does not see through terrain barriers or physical obstacles. Top players combine radar coverage with vision units, while utilizing stealth bots, cloaking bubbles, and radar-jamming fields to hide troop maneuvers.
   * Ballistics & Projectile Physics: Shots are physical entities—terrain height gives range and accuracy bonuses, while speed, angle, and line-of-fire dictate whether projectiles hit, miss, or hit allied units.
   * Plasma Deflectors & Choke Control: Deflectors ricochet cannon shells rather than absorbing them. Players build angled physical barricades (Dragon's Teeth) and deflector arrays to redirect enemy fire away from high-value infrastructure.

3. High-Level Micro & Automation Tricks:
   * Wait Queues & Synchronized Strikes: Pro players queue complex waypoint chains using the Wait (W) command—such as setting up multi-angle bomber runs or flank routes—and release them simultaneously for precise, undodgeable alphastrikes.
   * Fight-Move & Priority Queuing: Advanced command overlays allow players to set resource priority levels across factories and assign construction units to automatically reclaim battle debris while pushing.

1. OVERVIEW & INSPIRATIONS

This game design merges the mechanical scale and ballistics of Beyond All Reason
(BAR) with the persistent, massive-multiplayer world and economics of Foxhole.

* World: Set in a persistent, futuristic/robotic sci-fi war across a giant map.

* Perspective/Control: Isometric top-down perspective where players act as
  Field Commanders controlling unit formations, base macro, and frontline micro
  rather than single soldiers.

* Player Caps: Fixed server/hex limits for active commanders to maintain
  server performance, pathfinding integrity, and client FPS.

2. CORE LOGISTICS & ECONOMY LOOP

* Streamlined Streaming Economy: Dual-resource system based on Energy and Metal.

  * Energy: Generated via Solar, Wind, Fusion Cores, and Singularity Generators.

  * Metal: Harvested via extractors, synthesized via Matter Synthesizers, or
    reclaimed from destroyed wreckages on battlefields.

* Global/Faction Supply Pools:

  * Backline macro/industrial players mass-produce units and factories.

  * Completed units are uploaded directly into Clan or Global Faction Pools.

  * Frontline commanders log in, draw/requisition units from these pools, and
    immediately lead them into combat without waiting on build times.

3. LOG-OFF PERSISTENCE & ENTRENCHMENT PROTOCOL

To solve the issue of offline raiding and active player incentives:

* Log-Off Transformation / Liquidation:

  * When commanders log out, they can return mobile forces to local depots or
    convert active mobile units (tanks, assault bots) into static defenses
    (turrets, shield nodes, barricades).

* Autonomous AI Doctrines:

  * Remaining units transition into semi-automated defensive garrisons following
    preset AI doctrines (e.g., Patrol Line, Reclaim Field, Hold Position).

* Active Play Focus:

  * Active commanders focus heavily on micro-intensive operations (flanking,
    pushing, maneuvering).

  * Passive/offline state focuses on macro stability and defensive holding power.

4. RUNESCAPE-STYLE USE-BASED SKILL PROGRESSION

Commander progression is driven by performing specific operational actions on
the battlefield rather than global research timers.

* Earning XP:

  * Armor/Mechanized: Absorbing damage, executing flanks, keeping crews alive.

  * Siege/Artillery: Cracking fortifications, counter-battery fire, clearing lines.

  * Logistics/Macro: Reclaiming battle debris, power optimization, feeding supply.

  * Stealth/Air/Recon: Radar jamming, intelligence gathering, air intercepts.

* Mastery Rewards:

  * Unit Quality & Blueprints: High skill levels unlock access to higher tiers
    (T1 Light -> T2 Heavy -> T3 Experimental Super-Units).

  * Specialized Unit Caps: Overall unit cap increases, but extra cap is
    allocated toward mastered branches (e.g., 200 cap total, 150 reserved for Armor).

  * Anti-Griefing: Prevents novice players or alt accounts from wasting limited,
    high-value faction assets from the global pool.

5. SPATIAL LOGISTICS: WARP GATES & LAST-MILE DELIVERY

To move thousands of units across continent-scale maps efficiently while creating
emergent strategic meta-game targets:

* Long-Haul Transport (Quantum Warp Gates):

  * Faction super-structures that instantly warp massive unit batches between
    major logistics hubs and forward sector headquarters.

  * Requires massive energy input from backline power grids and calibration time.

* Last-Mile Delivery:

  * Heavy Mag-Trains: High-throughput, continuous supply along player-laid tracks.

  * Sub-Orbital Drop Pods: Rapid, pinpoint drops straight to frontline units,
    vulnerable to anti-air screens.

* High-Value Infrastructure Targets (HVT):

  * Partisan/Stealth players sabotage backline power grids feeding Warp Gates.

  * Ambush teams sever Mag-Rail lines to choke off supply to forward sieges.

  * Warp Jamming Towers force incoming drops off-target into defensive killzones.
